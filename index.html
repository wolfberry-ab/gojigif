How it works

Three steps,
one GIF.

No editor to learn and no export dialog to fill in. The GIF is ready the moment you stop.

01 / Choose

Draw a region.

Drag on the live preview and adjust the corners, or switch to the whole screen. GojiGif remembers your region for next time.

02 / Record

Press Record.

Move the region while you record to follow what you’re showing. Pause to skip the dull parts; paused time is left out of the GIF.

03 / Share

Stop, and it’s saved.

The GIF lands in your Downloads folder, or a folder you choose once. Play it back in the app or show it in Finder.

What you get

A GIF that plays
at the right speed.

Each frame keeps the time it was actually captured, so the GIF plays back as fast as it happened, not faster or slower.

  • Frame rate5–60 fps, 15 by default
  • ExtrasTimestamp, title card, cursor on or off
  • Long recordingsStop and save on their own when memory runs short

A preview you can trust.

The preview updates whenever your screen changes, at full resolution, so you know what you’ll get before you press Record.

Keeps itself out of the shot.

GojiGif hides its own window from the capture, so a whole-screen recording doesn’t show the recorder.

Picks up after sleep.

Lock your Mac or let the screensaver start, and the preview resumes when you come back.

Privacy

Your screen
stays yours.

Your screen shows messages, documents and passwords. GojiGif makes no network connections at all, so recordings and GIFs never leave your Mac. macOS asks for Screen Recording permission once, when you first record.

  • No account to create
  • No telemetry
  • No network connections
  • Runs in the macOS sandbox

Questions

Good to know.

Can’t find the answer?
Email us at info@wolfberry.se

Is GojiGif free?

Yes. There’s no account, trial or subscription, and the app has no paid features.

Which Macs does it run on?

Any Mac with macOS 12.3 Monterey or later, on Apple Silicon or Intel. One download covers both. macOS 12.3 is when Apple added the screen-capture technology GojiGif is built on.

Why does it need Screen Recording permission?

macOS requires it for any app that reads what’s on screen. GojiGif asks the first time you record, and you can turn it off in System Settings → Privacy & Security → Screen Recording. It asks for nothing else.

Where are my GIFs saved?

In your Downloads folder by default. Click Save to… to choose another folder; GojiGif remembers it. After each recording, the app tells you which folder the GIF went to.

Is there a Windows version?

It’s in development. We’ll release it once it has been tested properly. Follow the release feed to hear when it’s out.

Where can I find security updates?

Releases include SHA256 checksums, and macOS builds are signed by Wolfberry AB and notarized by Apple. Follow the security advisories or the release feed. Report a vulnerability to security@wolfberry.se; we aim to acknowledge reports within 72 hours. Machine-readable contact details are in security.txt.
