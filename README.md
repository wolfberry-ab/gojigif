# GojiGif

**Show it, don’t describe it.**

Record a region of your Mac’s screen and get an animated GIF, for a bug report, a changelog
or a quick “look at this”.

[Website](https://gojigif.wolfberry.se) · [Download](https://github.com/wolfberry-ab/gojigif/releases/latest) · [Privacy](https://gojigif.wolfberry.se/privacy-policy.html) · [License](LICENSE)

</div>

## Features

- **Region or whole screen.** Draw a selection on the live preview and adjust the corners,
  or record the entire display. Your region is remembered for next time.
- **Move while recording.** Drag the region to follow what you’re showing.
- **Pause and resume.** Paused time is left out of the GIF.
- **Real-time playback speed.** Each frame keeps the time it was captured, so the GIF plays
  as fast as it happened. 5–60 fps, 15 by default.
- **Extras.** An optional burned-in timestamp, a title card, and the cursor on or off.
- **Stays out of the shot.** GojiGif hides its own window from the capture.
- **Long recordings are safe.** When memory runs short, the recording stops and the GIF is
  saved, and the app tells you so.
- **Play it back** in the app, or show it in Finder.

## Install

Download `GojiGif-macos-<version>.dmg` from the
[latest release](https://github.com/wolfberry-ab/gojigif/releases/latest), open it and drag
**GojiGif** to Applications. A `.zip` of the same app is also attached.

- macOS 12.3 Monterey or later, on Apple Silicon or Intel (one universal app).
- Signed by Wolfberry AB and notarized by Apple. Each release lists SHA256 checksums.
- The first time you record, macOS asks for **Screen Recording** permission.

GIFs are saved to your **Downloads** folder. Click **Save to…** to choose another folder.

A Windows version is in development and will be released once it has been tested properly.

## Privacy & security

GojiGif is made by [Wolfberry AB](https://wolfberry.se), a security-focused software company
in Sweden.

- No telemetry, analytics, account or cloud. **The app makes no network connections.**
- Recordings and GIFs never leave your Mac.
- Runs in the macOS App Sandbox. The only sensitive permission it asks for is Screen Recording.
- Report a vulnerability to **security@wolfberry.se**, or open a private
  [security advisory](https://github.com/wolfberry-ab/gojigif/security/advisories/new).
  We aim to acknowledge reports within 72 hours.
